# Privacy Policy — Barakah Stories

**Last updated:** 19 September 2026

Barakah Stories is made by Klay Creative Lab LLC. This policy explains what the app does with your
information. The short version: the app is built to work without collecting anything about you,
and we do not operate a server that stores your data.

## What we collect

**Nothing is sent to us.** We do not run an account system in this version of the app, we do not
use analytics, we do not use advertising, and we have no way to identify you. We do not sell or
share personal information with anyone.

Everything you create in the app — your story designs, saved posts, recently opened posts, your
language, theme and prayer settings, and your Quran reading progress — is stored **only on your
device**, in the app's own storage. It is removed when you uninstall the app.

## Location

If you open the Prayer screen and choose to use your location, the app asks for location
permission. Your coordinates are used to:

- calculate prayer times and the direction of the Qibla, which happens **entirely on your device**;
  and
- look up the name of your city or area, so the screen can show where the times apply.

That place-name lookup is performed by your phone's own operating system service (Google Play
Services on Android, Apple's location services on iOS). Your coordinates are passed to that
service by the operating system, and its own privacy policy applies to that lookup. We never
receive your coordinates, and we do not store or transmit them anywhere else.

Location is only ever read while the app is open and you are using it. The app does not track you
in the background. You can refuse the permission and still use the app — choose one of the built-in
cities instead, and no location data is read at all. You can revoke the permission at any time in
your device settings.

The app also reads your phone's compass sensor to rotate the Qibla dial. That reading stays on the
device.

## Internet connections the app makes

The app needs the internet for one thing: downloading the hadith collections (Sahih al-Bukhari and
Sahih Muslim) the first time you open the Hadith section. They are fetched from the public jsDelivr
content delivery network and then cached on your device so they work offline afterwards.

As with visiting any website, the network that serves those files receives your device's IP address
in order to send a response. We do not control that network and we do not receive any information
about the request.

The Quran text and translation are bundled inside the app and require no connection at all.

## Sharing your stories

When you share a story, the app creates an image on your device and hands it to your phone's normal
share sheet. You choose where it goes — WhatsApp, another app, or your photo library. The image
never passes through us.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from them. There is
nothing to collect.

## Security

Because your content stays on your device, its security is governed by your device's own
protections, such as your screen lock and device encryption.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds features that handle data differently — for example optional
accounts that sync your saved posts between devices — this policy will be updated before that
version is released, and the date at the top will change.
